Good to know

Where do you deliver?

Kaduna next day, and Abuja and Lagos within 48 hours. Outside those cities, send us a message for a courier quote.

How long do the drinks keep?

Unopened and chilled, juice keeps for five days and fura for three. Please refrigerate on arrival and finish within a day of opening.

Do the drinks contain milk?

The three fura drinks contain cultured milk. The three juices are dairy-free.

Can I return the bottles?

Yes. Hand empty bottles back with your next delivery and we take ₦300 off your order.

Do you supply restaurants and shops?

We do, from twelve bottles a week. Use the form above and choose Wholesale.
